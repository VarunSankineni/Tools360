QR Code Generator

Turn a link or any text into a QR code you can download as a PNG.

    Your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to use QR Code Generator

    1. Type or paste your link.
    2. Choose a size and colors. Keep strong contrast so it scans well.
    3. Select Generate QR code and download the PNG.

    Questions and answers

    Do QR codes expire?

    No. The code simply contains your text, so it works as long as your link does.

    What should I put in it?

    Website links, Wi-Fi details, contact info or any short text.

    Is my text stored?

    No. It is created in your browser.
