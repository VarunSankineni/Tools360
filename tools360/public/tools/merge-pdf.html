Merge PDF

Combine two or more PDF files into one document. Reorder them first, all inside your browser.

Drop files here

or

PDF files, up to 50 MB each

    Your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to use Merge PDF

    1. Add two or more PDF files.
    2. Use the arrows to put them in the order you want.
    3. Select Merge PDF, then download the combined file.

    Questions and answers

    Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

    No. Merging happens in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

    Is there a file size limit?

    Each file can be up to 50 MB. Very large files depend on your device memory.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Not directly. Remove the password in the app that created the file, then merge.
