Compress PDF

Shrink large PDFs so they are easier to email and upload. Works best on scans and photo-heavy files.

Drop files here

or

PDF files, up to 50 MB each

    Compression turns each page into an image, so text will not be selectable afterwards. Text-only PDFs may not get smaller.

    Your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to use Compress PDF

    1. Add one or more PDF files.
    2. Choose a compression level.
    3. Select Compress PDF and compare the before and after sizes.

    Questions and answers

    Why can I not select text after compressing?

    To save space, pages are saved as images. Keep your original if you need searchable text.

    Why did my file not get smaller?

    Files that are already optimized cannot shrink further, so we return them unchanged.

    Is it private?

    Yes. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
