Pull out the pages you need, or turn every page into its own PDF.

    Your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to use Split PDF

    1. Add the PDF you want to split.
    2. Choose custom ranges like 1-3, 5 or every page separately.
    3. Select Split PDF and download your files.

    Questions and answers

    How do I write page ranges?

    Separate ranges with commas. For example 1-3, 5, 8-10 creates three files.

    Will I get many files?

    If you get more than one file, you can download them all together as a ZIP.

    Does this change the original?

    No. Your original file is never modified.
